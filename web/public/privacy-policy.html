AcademiX Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AcademiX Learning ("we", "us" or "our") handles information in the AcademiX mobile app and the associated school, teacher and student portals.

Information we handle

  • Account and school details: name, login ID, password credentials, school, branch, location, class, subjects, date of birth, gender, phone number and email address where collected.
  • Learning and school records: assignments, homework, submissions, answers, attachments, feedback, test attempts and results, report cards, attendance, lesson progress, rewards and app preferences.
  • Content and communications: uploaded curriculum documents, photos or other attachments, tutor questions and responses, support requests and related communications.
  • Voice and device information: audio you choose to record, transcripts, notification tokens, platform, time zone, notification preferences and technical request information.

Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information in questions, recordings, attachments or support messages.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify contact details, deliver learning materials, operate homework and tests, report progress, provide Sam and other learning features, send optional study and school notifications, respond to support requests and troubleshoot or secure the service.

A participating school and its authorized staff may access relevant records to administer classes, provide teaching and give support.

Sam, voice and uploaded material

Sam uses questions, conversation context and relevant class materials to generate responses. Audio you choose to record may be sent for speech-to-text processing. Generated speech may be sent back as audio. Uploaded images or attachments may be processed to identify, explain or grade learning content.

Conversation content may be stored so that the tutor conversation can continue. Microphone, camera and notification permissions are controlled through your device or browser settings; disabling a permission may make the corresponding feature unavailable.

Who receives information

Information may be shared with the participating school and authorized staff, and with providers used to operate AcademiX:

  • Supabase: database, account and application records.
  • Amazon Web Services: backend hosting and private database/backup storage.
  • OpenAI: tutor AI, voice-transcription and text-to-speech processing.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Android and iOS push notifications.
  • Twilio Verify: SMS verification for protected contact changes and account setup.

Transactional email delivery is not currently configured on the production server. Hugging Face processing is not currently configured. Providers receive the information needed for their service and remain responsible for their own processing, retention and deletion practices. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect users and the service.

Children and schools

AcademiX is used in educational settings and may handle children's information. Each participating school is responsible for providing required notices and obtaining parent or guardian consent where applicable. Parents and guardians may contact their school or AcademiX Learning for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Do not send passwords or verification codes in a privacy or support request.

Retention and deletion

School-managed information is retained while a school uses AcademiX, subject to the school's agreement and applicable legal, security or recordkeeping obligations. A pilot or contract period is not an automatic deletion deadline for every record.

When a school contract ends and the school does not continue, an authorized owner can permanently delete that school through the protected owner portal. The procedure is designed to remove the school account, related teacher and student accounts, and identified school-linked learning, support and device records. Exact school-name confirmation is required, and cleanup is verified before the procedure reports success.

Live verification: the permanent school-deletion procedure was tested against the live database using synthetic schools. The selected synthetic school and accounts were deleted, another synthetic school remained untouched, and all synthetic records were removed after testing.

Backups: AcademiX uses private database archives in Amazon S3 with a seven-day lifecycle and daily seven-day backend disk snapshots. Completed database archives are verified by catalog/checksum and independent download verification, and completed disk snapshots are maintained through an AWS lifecycle policy. These are recovery copies and are not deleted immediately when live records are removed. A live database deletion does not promise immediate deletion of provider copies, logs or recovery backups.

Older uploads: uploads and files that cannot be reliably matched to a school may require manual review. The asset registry is used to track newly uploaded homework and curriculum files for future school-deletion cleanup.

How to request deletion

Students and teachers should first contact their school administrator. You may also contact AcademiX Learning at jpr1521@gmail.com. Include the school name, account login ID or ID, and the account or data you want deleted. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Individual and school deletion requests may require a reasonable verification period. Deleted live records, retained backups and provider-held copies are handled separately according to the applicable deletion and retention process.

Security and international processing

AcademiX uses authenticated access, role controls, owner MFA and verified contact changes for protected school-profile actions. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. AWS, Supabase, OpenAI, Firebase and Twilio may process information in regions selected for those services. Appropriate transfer, retention and local-law safeguards should be applied through the school and operator.

Choices and requests

You can ask your school to correct school-managed details, discuss access to your information or request deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights. Device notification permissions can be changed in phone settings, and app preferences can be changed in the app.
